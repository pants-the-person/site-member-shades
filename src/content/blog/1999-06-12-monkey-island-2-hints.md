A friend asked for hints on Monkey Island 2 and I accidentally wrote a whole page. There are no story spoilers here, only puzzle nudges.

1. If you are stuck in the first town, talk to everyone twice. The second conversation is usually the useful one.
2. The spitting contest is about timing, not strength. Watch the judge, not the other contestants.
3. You can carry more than you think. Look at what is already in your inventory before you go looking for a new item.
4. If a puzzle seems to need money, it probably needs a favor instead.
5. Save before the crypt. Save after the crypt. Save in the crypt.
6. The answer is almost never "use the rubber chicken on the thing." Except when it is.

I am not telling you the monkey wrench puzzle. You have to earn that one.

If these hints are too vague, e-mail me and tell me where you are stuck. I will give you a worse hint.
