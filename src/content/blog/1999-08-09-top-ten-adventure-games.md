I have played a lot of adventure games. Here is the definitive list. It is correct. Do not argue with me in the guestbook. (You can argue with me in the guestbook.)

1. Monkey Island 2: LeChuck's Revenge
2. Day of the Tentacle
3. Grim Fandango
4. Sam & Max Hit the Road
5. Indiana Jones and the Fate of Atlantis
6. Broken Sword
7. The Secret of Monkey Island
8. Full Throttle
9. Beneath a Steel Sky
10. Loom

The order is based on puzzles, jokes, and how many times I had to draw a map on graph paper. Monkey Island 2 wins because the puzzle design is perfect and because the ending made me stare at the wall for ten minutes.

If your favorite is not on the list, it is probably number eleven.

Honorable mention: the SCUMM engine itself. It is the reason we can have nice things.
